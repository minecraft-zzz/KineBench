# Third-Party Notices

KineBench vendors third-party projects to make the evaluation stack reproducible. These projects keep their original licenses, copyright notices, and README files.

Vendored source trees:

- `third_party/ManiSkill-main`: ManiSkill.
- `third_party/FoundationPose-main`: FoundationPose, including its own third-party components such as nvdiffrast where present.
- `third_party/pyroki-main`: PyRoki.
- `third_party/MoGe-main`: MoGe, including its own third-party components such as utils3d where present.

Model weights, datasets, generated videos, logs, and runtime outputs are not part of the KineBench source release. They should be downloaded or produced separately and referenced through YAML configuration.
